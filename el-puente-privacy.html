Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

TAQUERIA EL PUENTE-1 LLC (“Taqueria El Puente,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information submitted through our website, giveaway and promotional forms, online interactions, phone calls, text messages, and other communications.

Information We Collect

We may collect:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Giveaway or promotional entry information
  • SMS marketing preferences and consent records
  • Communications with our business
  • Website usage information
  • Device and browser information

When you provide your mobile phone number through an SMS opt-in form, we may also maintain records relating to your consent, including the date and time of consent, the method through which consent was provided, and the consent language presented to you.

How We Use Information

We may use your information to:

  • Process giveaway and promotional entries
  • Communicate regarding giveaways or promotions you enter
  • Respond to questions or customer-service requests
  • Send promotional text messages when you have expressly opted in
  • Provide special offers, discounts, restaurant updates, and other marketing communications
  • Improve our website, promotions, and customer experience
  • Measure advertising and promotional performance
  • Maintain business and consent records

SMS Marketing

When you voluntarily opt in to SMS marketing, TAQUERIA EL PUENTE-1 LLC may send recurring promotional text messages to the mobile number you provide.

Messages may include special offers, discounts, promotions, giveaways, restaurant news, and other marketing updates.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

Consent to receive marketing text messages is not a condition of purchase.

You may withdraw your consent at any time by replying STOP to a marketing text message.

Mobile Information and SMS Consent

We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile phone number or SMS opt-in data and consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.

We may use service providers that assist us in operating our SMS messaging program and other business systems. These providers may process information only as necessary to provide services on our behalf.

SMS opt-in data and consent will not be sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We may use cookies, pixels, analytics tools, and similar technologies to understand website activity, measure advertising performance, improve our services, and maintain website functionality.

These technologies may collect information such as your IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, and referring website.

You may manage cookies through your browser settings, although disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

Information Sharing

We may share information with service providers that help us operate our business, such as website hosting, communications, SMS messaging, analytics, payment processing, and other operational services.

These providers may use information only as necessary to provide services on our behalf.

We do not sell personal information.

For clarity, mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in data, and SMS consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect personal information. However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information by contacting us.

You may opt out of promotional text messages at any time by replying STOP.
